01 / Our development

Built on specialist expertise.

Each milestone adds a distinct capability to our manufacturing network.

2010↗

MDHI founded

Modern Heavy Industries (Taicang) Co., Ltd. was founded, establishing the structural steel and industrial fabrication capabilities behind the network.

2017↗

MMEC founded

Modern Modular Engineering & Construction Co., Ltd. was founded. Its port-adjacent modular yard added integrated-module capability to the network.

2025↗

MEEM facility opens

A dedicated manufacturing facility for HRSG pressure parts and air-cooled heat exchangers opened, completing the current four-product-line network.
