Privacy

Any personal data submitted through contact forms or emails is used solely to respond to your enquiry and to route it to the relevant operating company. Data is not used for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Enquiry records are retained only as long as necessary to handle the request and satisfy applicable business or legal record-keeping requirements, typically for the duration of the related commercial relationship plus any statutory period.
