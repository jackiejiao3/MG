MDHI · Taicang

Modern Heavy Industries (Taicang) Co., Ltd. (MDHI)

Structural steel fabrication
American Institute of Steel Construction (AISC) building fabrication and coating endorsement, Los Angeles Department of Building and Safety (LADBS) fabricator approval, Canadian Welding Bureau (CWB) certification to CSA W47.1, EN 1090, EN 13084-7 and ISO 3834-2.
Management systems
ISO 9001 for quality, ISO 14001 for environmental management and ISO 45001 for occupational health and safety.

MDHI’s quality page marks some entries as being renewed. Confirm the latest document and execution class with MDHI when these are specified.
