01 / Quality & responsible operations

Responsible operations.
A practical management approach.

Clear responsibilities, risk assessment, workplace monitoring and continual improvement provide a practical framework for responsible manufacturing.

Step 01 / Governance and responsibility

Bring responsibility into daily work.

Clear responsibilities and coordination across quality, technical, production and HSE teams connect responsible manufacturing, environmental protection and employee well-being with everyday decisions.

Management priorities

  • ✓Clear responsibilities
  • ✓Cross-functional coordination
  • ✓Continual improvement

Step 02 / Risk assessment and control

Identify hazards. Define the controls.

Identify workplace hazards, assess occupational health and safety risks and define practical controls. Risk-based planning helps teams prepare for routine work and unexpected situations.

Management priorities

  • ✓Hazard identification
  • ✓Health and safety risk assessment
  • ✓Control measures

Step 03 / Workplace and environmental monitoring

Look closely at the working environment.

Workplace monitoring focuses on chemical hazards and physical factors, while environmental monitoring considers water discharge, emissions and noise. Occupational health surveillance helps identify concerns and supports appropriate action.

Management priorities

  • ✓Workplace chemical and physical factors
  • ✓Water, emissions and noise
  • ✓Occupational health examinations

Step 04 / Operational improvement

Reduce errors, waste and resource use.

Efficient engineering, careful procurement and resource-conscious production help reduce errors and manufacturing waste. Improvement priorities include energy-efficient equipment, emission control and tracking water use.

Management priorities

  • ✓Efficient engineering
  • ✓Resource-efficient equipment
  • ✓Waste and emission reduction

Improve the way work is done

Efficiency with responsibility.

Efficient engineering, careful procurement and improved equipment support fewer errors, less manufacturing waste and more responsible resource use.

02 / Health, safety & environment

Care beyond
the finished product.

Protecting people starts with understanding workplace risks. Training, emergency preparedness and responsible resource use support safer, more sustainable operations.

01

Occupational health & safety

Protect people.

Preventing harm is the priority. Risk assessment, occupational health surveillance and monitoring of workplace hazards help protect employees throughout daily operations.

  • Harm prevention
  • Occupational health
  • Workplace monitoring
02

Training & emergency preparedness

Prepare. Train. Respond.

Training in workshop and fire safety, environmental incidents and spill response helps teams understand their responsibilities. Practical preparation and clear response plans support effective action when an emergency occurs.

  • Workshop & fire safety
  • Emergency preparedness
  • Spill response
03

Environmental protection

Use resources wisely.

Use energy and water efficiently, control emissions and reduce manufacturing waste. Recycling, material reuse and responsible handling of hazardous waste help limit the environmental impact of operations.

  • Energy efficiency
  • Emission reduction
  • Waste treatment
