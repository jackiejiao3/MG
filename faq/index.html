Group & Companies

What is Modern Group, and which companies operate under its brand?

Modern Group is the shared commercial brand for three specialist manufacturers in Jiangsu, China. The companies and their manufacturing scopes are listed below.

Modern Heavy Industries (Taicang) Co., Ltd. (MDHI)
Structural steel fabrication.
Modern Modular Engineering & Construction Co., Ltd. (MMEC)
Structural steel fabrication and modular plant manufacturing.
Modern Energy Equipment Manufacturing Co., Ltd. (MEEM)
Heat recovery steam generator (HRSG) pressure parts and air-cooled heat exchangers.

Related information: MDHI · MMEC · MEEM

Which Modern Group companies fabricate structural steel?

Two operating companies fabricate structural steel. The appropriate operating company is determined by the project scope.

Modern Heavy Industries (Taicang) Co., Ltd. (MDHI)
Based in Taicang. Structural steel for buildings, infrastructure and industrial projects, with detailing, fabrication, coating and assembly.
Modern Modular Engineering & Construction Co., Ltd. (MMEC)
Based in Zhangjiagang. Heavy structural steel fabrication and steelwork for modular plant packages.

Related information: MDHI · MMEC · Structural Steel

Which company manufactures modular plants, and what can it supply?

Modern Modular Engineering & Construction Co., Ltd. (MMEC) manufactures modular plants and process modules in Zhangjiagang. Its manufacturing scope may include structural fabrication, pipe spool fabrication, module assembly, blasting and coating. MMEC confirms the scope of supply for each enquiry.

Related information: MMEC · Modular Plants

What facilities does MMEC use for modular plant manufacturing?

Modern Modular Engineering & Construction Co., Ltd. (MMEC) operates a 77,000 m² covered workshop and a 166,000 m² modular yard in Zhangjiagang. The modular yard is adjacent to Gangxin Port.

Related information: MMEC

Which HRSG pressure parts does MEEM manufacture?

Modern Energy Equipment Manufacturing Co., Ltd. (MEEM) manufactures heat recovery steam generator (HRSG) pressure parts, including finned tubes, headers, manifolds, harps and modules. For a quotation, specify the components or assemblies required, design conditions, material specifications and any inspection or certification requirements.

Related information: MEEM · HRSG Pressure Parts

What types of air-cooled heat exchangers does MEEM manufacture?

Modern Energy Equipment Manufacturing Co., Ltd. (MEEM) manufactures air-cooled heat exchanger (ACHE) equipment for power generation and process applications. Its portfolio includes air-finned coolers, air-cooled condensers, modular air-cooled heat exchangers, hot air recirculation air-cooled heat exchangers and tube bundles.

Related information: MEEM · Air-Cooled Heat Exchangers

Which operating company manufactures pressure vessels?

Modern Energy Equipment Manufacturing Co., Ltd. (MEEM) manufactures pressure vessels for industrial and process applications. Send MEEM your equipment specifications, design conditions and required code so it can confirm the fabrication and certification scope.

Related information: MEEM pressure vessels · MEEM certificate summary

Can MMEC arrange transport and port load-out for completed modules?

Yes. Modern Modular Engineering & Construction Co., Ltd. (MMEC) can arrange transport by self-propelled modular transporter (SPMT) and port load-out for completed modules. MMEC confirms the transport and load-out scope for each enquiry.

Related information: MMEC · Modular Plants

Can MMEC coordinate export shipping and documentation?

Yes. Modern Modular Engineering & Construction Co., Ltd. (MMEC) describes port-side and Free on Board (FOB) delivery options. It can also help obtain freight quotations, book vessels, arrange insurance and provide certificates of origin. Confirm the services and delivery terms for your shipment with MMEC.

Related information: MMEC logistics

Can the operating company arrange export packing and sea fastening?

Yes. The responsible operating company can coordinate export packing, corrosion protection, protection during lifting and sea fastening. The arrangements depend on the shipment’s dimensions, weight, destination and mode of transport.

What information should I include in a request for quotation?

A request for quotation should cover three areas: project and delivery details, technical scope, and inspection and documentation requirements.

Project and delivery
Product line, project region, quantities and delivery destination.
Technical scope
Drawings and applicable standards. If drawings are incomplete, include available photos or sample details.
Inspection and documentation
Inspection requirements and any certificates required for the project.
