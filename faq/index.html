What is Modern Group?

Modern Group is the short name for Modern Heavy Industries Group. The group connects three specialist manufacturers in Jiangsu, China, whose work includes fabricating structural steel, building modular plants, and manufacturing heat recovery steam generator (HRSG) pressure parts and air-cooled heat exchangers.

Learn more →
What is the difference between Modern Group and MDHI, MMEC and MEEM?

Modern Group is the name MDHI, MMEC and MEEM use to present their combined capabilities and coordinate enquiries. For each project, the relevant operating company provides the quotation, signs the contract and takes responsibility for quality, applicable certifications and delivery.

Learn more →
Which company should I contact first?

Start with the product scope. Contact MDHI or MMEC for structural steel, depending on the project requirements, MMEC for modular plants, and MEEM for HRSG pressure parts and air-cooled heat exchangers. If you are unsure, send one enquiry to Modern Group for coordination.

Where are the Modern Group manufacturing sites?

MDHI: 169 Longjiang Road, Taicang. MMEC: 2669 Yanjiang Road, Zhangjiagang. MEEM: 1 Duanshan Road, Zhangjiagang. The group also has an engineering centre in Shanghai at 183 Kaibin Road, Building B.

Which company manufactures structural steel?

MDHI in Taicang and MMEC in Zhangjiagang manufacture structural steel. Both fabricate project-specific packages, and enquiries are routed by product scope to the company with the relevant responsibility.

Learn more →
What is the difference between MDHI and MMEC for structural steel?

Both manufacture structural steel. MDHI is in Taicang and focuses on structural steel; MMEC is in Zhangjiagang and also manufactures modular plants. Enquiries are routed by product scope.

Learn more →
Which company manufactures modular plants?

MMEC in Zhangjiagang manufactures modular plants, including process modules, process skids and preassembled pipe racks. It provides integrated module assembly and port-oriented logistics, about 1 km from the Gangxin Heavy Equipment Terminal.

Learn more →
Which company manufactures HRSG pressure parts and air-cooled heat exchangers?

MEEM in Zhangjiagang manufactures HRSG pressure parts and air-cooled heat exchangers. It is the group's dedicated energy-equipment facility for power generation and process applications.

Learn more →
What HRSG components does MEEM manufacture?

Finned tubes, headers, harps and platens, and bundles and modules. MEEM manufactures HRSG pressure-part assemblies for heat recovery steam generator applications.

Learn more →
What types of air-cooled heat exchangers does MEEM manufacture?

Air finned coolers, air cooled condensers, modular air-cooled heat exchangers, hot air recirculation air-cooled heat exchangers, and tube bundles.

How close are your factories to the port?

Very close. MMEC is about 1 km from the Gangxin Heavy Equipment Terminal, MEEM about 2 km, and MDHI about 10 km from Taicang Port. All three sites are on the Yangtze River.

How do you ship large or oversized items?

Large and oversized items ship through Yangtze River ports close to the factories. Lifting, transport and packing plans are prepared for oversized items with the operating company.

Can MMEC arrange module transport and port load-out?

Yes. MMEC can coordinate transport by self-propelled modular transporter (SPMT) and port load-out. It also offers portside or Free on Board (FOB) delivery options and can assist with vessel booking and export documentation, depending on the agreed scope.

Do you handle export packing and sea fastening?

Yes. Export packing, rust prevention, lifting protection and sea fastening are arranged with the operating company, according to size, weight, destination and transport method.
