Frequently asked questions

Answers before you enquire.

Ten questions procurement and project teams ask most about Modern Group, MDHI, MMEC and MEEM. Each answer starts with a direct answer, then a link to the page with the detail.

1. What is Modern Group?

Modern Group is a shared commercial identity connecting three specialist manufacturing companies in Jiangsu, China: MDHI, MMEC and MEEM. Together they cover four product lines: structural steel, modular plants, HRSG pressure parts and air-cooled heat exchangers. Modern Group is a commercial brand, not a separate legal entity, so contracts, certifications and project delivery remain with the relevant operating company.

Modern Group is the shared commercial identity for MDHI, MMEC and MEEM. Read more on the About Modern Group page and the Companies & Locations page.

2. Which company manufactures structural steel?

Structural steel is manufactured by two Modern Group companies: MDHI (Modern Heavy Industries (Taicang) Co., Ltd.) in Taicang and MMEC (Modern Modular Engineering & Construction (Suzhou) Co., Ltd.) in Zhangjiagang. Both fabricate project-specific structural steel packages, and enquiries are routed by product scope so the enquiry reaches the company with the relevant manufacturing responsibility.

See Product Portfolio and Companies & Locations.

3. Which company manufactures modular plants?

Modular plants are manufactured by MMEC, Modern Modular Engineering & Construction (Suzhou) Co., Ltd., based in Zhangjiagang, Jiangsu. MMEC provides integrated module assembly and port-oriented logistics, including a riverfront yard close to the Gangxin Heavy Equipment Terminal, which is about 1 km from the MMEC site.

See Product Portfolio.

4. Which company manufactures HRSG pressure parts and air-cooled heat exchangers?

HRSG pressure parts and air-cooled heat exchangers are manufactured by MEEM, Modern Energy Equipment Manufacturing Co., Ltd., based in Zhangjiagang, Jiangsu. MEEM is the group's dedicated facility for energy equipment, covering HRSG pressure-part assemblies and air-cooled heat exchanger equipment for power generation and process applications.

See Product Portfolio.

5. Where are the Modern Group manufacturing sites?

MDHI is in Taicang at 169 Longjiang Road, Taicang Port Development Zone. MMEC is in Zhangjiagang at 2669 Yanjiang Road, Daxin Town. MEEM is in Zhangjiagang at 1 Duanshan Road, Deji Subdistrict. The group also has an engineering centre in Shanghai at 183 Kaibin Road, Building B. All three manufacturing sites are in Jiangsu, China, close to Yangtze River ports.

See the map and site details on Companies & Locations.

7. Who holds the certifications and quality responsibility?

Certifications and quality responsibility are held by the relevant operating company, not by Modern Group as a commercial brand. Each company applies the group's quality, health, safety and environment standards within its own manufacturing scope. For a specific certificate, its scope and validity, ask the operating company directly and request the verification link before placing an order.

See Quality & HSE. Note: specific certificate names are not listed here until Quality confirms the current list with validity dates and verification links.

8. How are large modules and steel structures shipped?

Large items are shipped through Yangtze River ports close to the manufacturing sites. MMEC is about 1 km from the Gangxin Heavy Equipment Terminal and MEEM about 2 km away; MDHI is about 10 km from Taicang Port. The nearest international airport is Shanghai Pudong, about 95 km from MDHI and about 230 km from MMEC and MEEM. Export packing and sea fastening are arranged with the operating company.

See Companies & Locations. Distances are straight-line reference distances from the site map, not road distances.

9. What is the difference between Modern Group and MDHI, MMEC and MEEM?

Modern Group is the shared commercial identity for the three companies; it is not a separate incorporated legal entity. MDHI, MMEC and MEEM are the separate operating companies that quote, contract, certify and deliver. MDHI focuses on structural steel, MMEC on structural steel and modular plants, and MEEM on HRSG pressure parts and air-cooled heat exchangers.

See About Modern Group.

The enquiry is directed to the operating company with the relevant manufacturing scope, which can then quote, contract and deliver. Use the Contact page enquiry form, or email the relevant company directly.
