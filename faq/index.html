What is Modern Group?

Modern Group is a shared commercial identity connecting MDHI, MMEC and MEEM, three specialist manufacturers in Jiangsu, China. Together they cover structural steel, modular plants, HRSG pressure parts and air-cooled heat exchangers.

Learn more →
Which company manufactures structural steel?

MDHI in Taicang and MMEC in Zhangjiagang manufacture structural steel. Both fabricate project-specific packages, and enquiries are routed by product scope to the company with the relevant responsibility.

Learn more →
Which company manufactures modular plants?

MMEC in Zhangjiagang manufactures modular plants. It provides integrated module assembly and port-oriented logistics, about 1 km from the Gangxin Heavy Equipment Terminal.

Learn more →
Which company manufactures HRSG pressure parts and air-cooled heat exchangers?

MEEM in Zhangjiagang manufactures HRSG pressure parts and air-cooled heat exchangers. It is the group's dedicated energy-equipment facility for power generation and process applications.

Learn more →
Where are the Modern Group manufacturing sites?

MDHI: 169 Longjiang Road, Taicang. MMEC: 2669 Yanjiang Road, Zhangjiagang. MEEM: 1 Duanshan Road, Zhangjiagang. The group also has an engineering centre in Shanghai at 183 Kaibin Road, Building B.

How do I send an enquiry to Modern Group?

Start with the product scope on the Contact page. Enquiries go to jackie.jo@mdhichina.com, or directly to MDHI (info@mdhiglobal.com), MMEC (build@mmecglobal.com), or MEEM (info@m-energy.com).

Who holds the certifications and quality responsibility?

Certifications are held by the operating company, not by Modern Group as a brand. Ask the relevant company directly for a certificate's scope, validity and verification link before ordering.

Learn more →
How are large modules and steel structures shipped?

Large items ship via Yangtze River ports: MMEC is about 1 km from Gangxin Terminal, MEEM about 2 km, and MDHI about 10 km from Taicang Port. Export packing and sea fastening are arranged with the operating company.

Learn more →
What is the difference between Modern Group and MDHI, MMEC and MEEM?

Modern Group is the shared commercial identity, not a separate legal entity. MDHI, MMEC and MEEM are the operating companies that quote, contract, certify and deliver.

How do I request a quotation?

Send the product line, project region, drawings, standards and quantities. Your enquiry goes to the operating company with the relevant scope, which then quotes, contracts and delivers.
