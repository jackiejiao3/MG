Egypt Power Projects — Outstanding Performance Recognition

MDHI received recognition in March 2017 for outstanding manufacturing and supply performance on the Beni Suef and New Capital power projects in Egypt. The award specifically records main steel and casing for 8 heat recovery steam generators (HRSGs), plus ducting and stacks for 12 HRSGs.

The wider project reference covers Beni Suef Power Station (4.8 GW, 8 HRSGs) and New Capital Power Station (2.4 GW, 4 HRSGs), with NEM Energy B.V. listed as the customer. MDHI’s recorded supply scope included modular ducts and casing, main steel, stacks, stair towers, platforms, ladders, grating and handrails.

For EPC procurement teams, this reference brings together HRSG steelwork, gas-path components and access structures within one project supply scope. It offers relevant experience for evaluating fabrication package coverage and coordinating interfaces between structural steel, ducting, casing and access systems.
