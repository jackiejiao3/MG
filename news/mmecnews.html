Modern’s Modular Yard Under Construction

2020-02-25

*MODULAR WORKSHOP CONSTRUCTION*

Workshop: MODERN MODULAR ENGINEERING & CONSTRUCTION CO.

Industry: Modular Fabrication & Construction

Yard Area: 166,000m2 / 1,786,809sq.ft.

Supply Scope: Modular Pre-fabrication, Steel Structure Fabrication, Piping Works, Electrical & Instrumentation, Insulation Works

Founded: April 1, 2017

Location: Gangxin Heavy Equipment Port, Zhangjiagang, Jiangsu, China

Covered Area: 75,500m2 / 823,500sq.ft.

Products: Wellpad Skids, Manifold Skids, Separation Skids, Water Treatment, Complex Process, Pipe Racks, Offshore Structures, Air Cooled Condensers, Plant Structural Steel, Equipment Supports, Platform & Grating, Ladder & Handrail

Modern Heavy Industries (MDHI) has founded a new modular fabrication division, Modern Modular Engineering & Construction (MMEC). Founded in 2017, ground was broken in 2019 and the yard will start producing in the summer of 2020. There are many advantages to front-end engineering to use modular solutions, such as increasing project execution speed, reducing site labor, reducing shipments, reducing site inventory, better constructability, reduced handling, pre-fabrication assembly & testing, etc. MMEC will serve the oil & gas, mining, HRSG, and general construction industries. Services will provide detailed design, superior project management, modular construction, piping, E&I, insulation, etc. for a wide range of process modules.
