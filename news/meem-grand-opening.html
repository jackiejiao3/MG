28 October 2025/MEEM

Modern Group Opens Its Third Manufacturing Facility

A new purpose-built operation in Zhangjiagang, Jiangsu, dedicated exclusively to energy equipment fabrication.

1 Duanshan Road, Deji Subdistrict, Zhangjiagang City, Jiangsu Province 215635
A New Chapter for Modern Group

Built for the future of energy equipment manufacturing

Modern Group is pleased to announce the opening of Modern Energy Equipment Manufacturing Co., Ltd. (MEEM), the Group's third and latest manufacturing facility.

Located in Zhangjiagang, Jiangsu, MEEM is dedicated exclusively to energy equipment fabrication. The purpose-built operation expands the Group's capacity for boiler pressure parts, finned tubing, air-cooled heat exchangers, and process piping while strengthening its ability to support customers and energy projects worldwide.

Dedicated workshop areas, modern production infrastructure, and an experienced workforce support safe, efficient fabrication, assembly, testing, and quality control across the new facility.
